Tempo Calendar — Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Tempo Calendar ("Tempo") is a browser extension that shows your Google Calendar and schedules tasks into your free time. This policy explains what data Tempo handles and where that data goes.

The short version

  • Tempo has no servers. The developer never receives, sees or stores your data.
  • Your data travels only between your browser and Google, and only when you connect your Google account.
  • There is no analytics, tracking, advertising or selling of data, and no data is shared with anyone else.

What Tempo handles and why

DataWhyWhere it goes
Your Google account email addressShown in Settings so you know which account is connectedRead from Google; stored only in your browser
Your calendar list and calendar events (titles, times, locations, guests, descriptions, reminders)To display your calendar, create and edit events at your request, and find free time for tasksRead from and written to your own Google Calendar
Tasks you create (name, deadline, rank, session settings, notes, done/undone status)To schedule task sessionsStored in your browser, and written into the matching session events in your own Google Calendar, so other browsers where you use Tempo can load them
Google sign-in tokenTo talk to Google Calendar on your behalfStored only in your browser's extension storage; revoked when you click Disconnect
Settings, local events and cached dataSo the app works and remembers your preferencesStored only in your browser's extension storage
Backups of your tasks and settings (only if you turn on Drive backups)So you can restore them on any deviceA hidden app folder in your Google Drive that only Tempo can read; Tempo can't see your other Drive files. The last 14 backups are kept.

Tempo contacts only these addresses: accounts.google.com (sign-in), www.googleapis.com (Calendar API, and Drive API for backups if you turn them on), openidconnect.googleapis.com (your email address) and oauth2.googleapis.com (revoking access when you disconnect).

If you never connect a Google account, Tempo works fully offline and no data leaves your browser.

Google user data

Tempo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the calendar and scheduling features you see in the extension. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by humans.

Your control

  • Disconnect (Settings → Google Calendar) revokes Tempo's access to your Google account. You can also remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling Tempo deletes everything it stored in your browser.
  • Task sessions Tempo wrote to Google Calendar are ordinary events in your calendar. You can delete them there, or use Settings → Google Calendar → *Remove stray task events*.
  • Backups (Settings → Data) are files you download yourself. Tempo never uploads them anywhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the extension update and the effective date above changes.
